How to Make a Delicious Cheeseburger

A cheeseburger is a delicious sandwich meal made with a juicy beef patty, melted cheese, fresh vegetables, and flavorful sauces, all served between soft burger buns. It is a popular meal enjoyed around the world.

Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • 200g ground beef
  • 2 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • Fresh lettuce leaves
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Preparation

  1. Place the ground beef in a bowl and season with salt and black pepper.
  2. Shape the seasoned beef into round burger patties.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  4. Place the beef patties in the pan and cook until browned and thoroughly cooked, turning them halfway through.
  5. Place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty during the final minute of cooking and allow it to melt.
  6. Cut the burger buns in half and lightly toast the cut sides.
  7. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the inside of the buns.
  8. Place lettuce, tomato slices, onion, and the cooked beef patty on the bottom bun.
  9. Cover with the top bun and serve warm.
