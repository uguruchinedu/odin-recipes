How to Prepare Nigerian Jollof Rice

Nigerian Jollof Rice is a popular West African rice dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, and a blend of spices. It is commonly served at parties, celebrations, and family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Fresh Tomatoes
  • Tomato Paste
  • Pepper
  • Onions
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Chicken Stock/Beef/Fish (Proteins)
  • Vegetables

Preparation

  1. Boil the proteins with pepper and seasoning
  2. Parboil and wash the rice thoroughly
  3. Blend the tomatoes, pepper and inions
  4. Heat vegetable oil in a pot
  5. Add the blended mixture
  6. Allow the sauce to cook prperly
  7. Add seasoning,salt and the boild proteins
  8. Add the rice and stir well
  9. Cover and cook the rice until is soft and water dries
  10. Add vegetables
  11. Serve hot and enjoy
