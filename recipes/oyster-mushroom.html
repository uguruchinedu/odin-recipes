How to Prepare Oyster-Mushroom

Oyster mushrooms are delicious edible mushrooms known for their soft texture and delicate flavour. They can be prepared by sautéing, frying, grilling, or adding them to soups and sauces. They are a popular ingredient in many dishes and can be enjoyed as a nutritious meal.

Ingredients

  • Fresh oyster mushrooms
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon of ground pepper
  • 1 seasoning cube
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh vegetables (optional)

Preparation

  1. Clean and prepare the oyster mushrooms.
  2. Prepare the other ingredients.
  3. Heat a pan and add the required oil.
  4. Add the mushrooms and cook until properly done.
  5. Add the remaining ingredients and stir well.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy.
