How to make a Delicious Chicken Shawarma

Chicken Shawarma is a popular and delicious Middle Eastern-inspired street food made with tender, well-seasoned chicken, fresh vegetables, creamy sauce, and soft shawarma bread. The chicken is marinated with a combination of spices and seasonings, then cooked until juicy and flavorful before being sliced into thin pieces.

The chicken is combined with crunchy cabbage, carrots, and cucumber, then topped with a rich mixture of mayonnaise and ketchup. Everything is wrapped tightly in soft shawarma bread and lightly grilled or toasted until the outside is warm and slightly crispy.

Chicken Shawarma is easy to prepare at home and makes a satisfying meal for lunch, dinner, or a quick snack. It can also be customized with different vegetables, sauces, and seasonings according to personal taste.

Ingredients

For the Chicken

  • 1 kg chicken breast or chicken thighs
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon seasoning powder
  • Salt to taste

For the Vegetables

  • ½ medium-sized cabbage
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 small onion

For the Shawarma Sauce

  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon sweet chili sauce
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder

For the Wrap

  • Shawarma bread or flatbread
  • Cheese (optional)

Preparation

Step 1. Prepare and Season the Chicken

  1. Wash and clean the chicken properly, then pat it dry with a clean kitchen towel
  2. Cut the chicken into thin strips so that it will cook evenly and be easy to wrap
  3. Place the chicken in a bowl and add paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, curry powder, thyme, black pepper, seasoning powder, and salt
  4. Add the vegetable oil and mix everything thoroughly until the chicken is evenly coated with the seasoning
  5. Cover the bowl and allow the chicken to marinate for at least 30 minutes so the flavors can develop

Step 2. Cook the Chicken

  1. Heat a frying pan or grill over medium heat
  2. Add the marinated chicken and cook until it is properly done and lightly browned on the outside
  3. Turn the chicken occasionally while cooking to ensure that all sides are evenly cooked
  4. Remove the cooked chicken from the heat and allow it to rest for a few minutes before using it

Step 3. Prepare the Vegetables

  1. Wash the cabbage, carrots, cucumber, and onion thoroughly
  2. Slice the cabbage into thin strips
  3. Peel and grate or thinly slice the carrots
  4. Slice the cucumber and onion into thin pieces
  5. Keep the prepared vegetables aside in a clean bowl

Step 4. Prepare the Shawarma Sauce

  1. In a clean bowl, combine the mayonnaise and ketchup
  2. Add the sweet chili sauce and garlic powder
  3. Mix thoroughly until the sauce becomes smooth and evenly combined

Step 5. Assemble the Shawarma

  1. Place one shawarma bread or flatbread on a clean, flat surface
  2. Spread some of the prepared sauce over the center of the bread
  3. Add some cabbage, carrots, cucumber, and onion
  4. Place a generous portion of the cooked chicken on top of the vegetables
  5. Add some cheese if desired, then drizzle a little more sauce over the filling

Step 6. Roll and Toast the Shawarma

  1. Fold the sides of the bread inward to prevent the filling from falling out
  2. Roll the bread tightly from one end to the other
  3. Place the wrapped shawarma on a heated pan or sandwich toaster
  4. Toast it lightly on both sides until the bread becomes warm and slightly crispy
  5. Remove from the heat, cut it in half if desired, and serve immediately
